A little about me

The art of paying attention.

My work lives in the in-between: the exhale before a ceremony begins, the way afternoon light finds a kitchen table, a hand reaching for another hand.

With a documentary eye and an editorial sensibility, I create photographs that feel effortless now and meaningful for years to come.
